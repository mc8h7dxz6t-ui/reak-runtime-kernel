# Competitive responsibility map

Each row is where that product’s own documentation or public description says the job stops. Overlap means a buyer can already point at that product for part of the same sentence.

| Party | What they actually solve | Where responsibility ends | Assumption they leave with the customer | Missing, by their own account or by the incident pattern | Natural join |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft AGT | Policy before an MCP or tool call; allow, deny, approval callback, redaction, audit sink. | One call. Not a sequence. Not proof the external system applied the effect. | The tool server and the policy author are honest enough for the decision. | Sequence and intent checks are “on the roadmap.” ([AGT post](https://developer.microsoft.com/blog/securing-mcp-a-control-plane-for-agent-tool-execution/)) | In front of any tool the agent can call. |
| Okta, Entra, Ping, CyberArk, Delinea, StrongDM | Who is allowed to reach an account, vault, or privileged session. | The session or the token. Not whether a later business effect was unique or uncertain. | Once access is granted, the application uses it correctly. | Not an execution record of external effects. | Identity for the caller that hits a gateway. |
| Cedar, OPA, Styra | A deterministic allow or deny from a policy and an input. | The decision. Enforcement is the caller’s job. AWS Verified Permissions and AGT are enforcement hosts, not Cedar itself. | Inputs to the decision are the inputs that matter. | No durability of “we sent this,” no external observation. | The rule language inside a gateway. |
| Temporal | Durable workflow history; activity retry; saga compensation. | At-least-once activities unless the user sets one attempt. Compensation is another activity and can itself fail; Temporal tells the user to pause for an operator. ([Saga guide](https://docs.temporal.io/guides/saga-pattern), [idempotency post](https://temporal.io/blog/idempotency-and-durable-execution)) | Activities are idempotent, or one attempt is acceptable, including zero. | Does not refuse to retry when the external outcome is unknown. The default is the opposite. | The history of steps, if someone else forbids a second side effect. |
| Camunda, Conductor, Step Functions | Orchestration, retries, callbacks, human tasks. | Step Functions Standard claims exactly-once workflow execution unless Retry is set, and says that suits payments. The Durable Execution SDK then says that is not end-to-end exactly-once without no-retry. ([workflow types](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/step-functions/latest/dg/choosing-workflow-type.html), [SDK idempotency](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/durable-execution/patterns/best-practices/idempotency/)) | The integrated service, or the user’s activity code, is safe to run again. | Acknowledgement loss is a retry or a timeout, not a frozen unknown. | Same as Temporal. |
| Datadog, Honeycomb, Splunk, New Relic | Telemetry the application emits. | They show what the application reported. | The application logged the truth. | A log the process never wrote, or a log written after the fact, is invisible. | Detection, not authority to send. |
| Drata, Vanta, AuditBoard | Collect evidence that controls exist, for a questionnaire or an auditor. | The control library and the uploaded artifact. Complair, writing about AI Act logs, says the logs stay in the customer’s infrastructure; the product tracks that retention happened. ([Complair on Article 12](https://complair.eu/docs/article-12-logging)) | The customer’s systems produced the artifact. | Not a witness of an external effect. | The place a bounded report is stored for an auditor, after someone else produced it. |
| LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, OpenAI Agents SDK, Google ADK | How agents plan and call tools. | The framework runs the loop. Governance is a host or gateway concern; Microsoft says MCP SDKs delegate that to the host. | The model’s next call is acceptable if the tool exists. | No durable unknown, no independent qualification. | The thing a gateway sits in front of. |

Identity products and policy languages are neighbours, not substitutes. Workflow products are the closest substitute for “remember what was attempted.” Observability and GRC products are the closest substitute for “show an auditor something.” None of those pages claim: if we are not sure the outside world committed, we will not send again, and a party who does not run the system can recompute that fact.
